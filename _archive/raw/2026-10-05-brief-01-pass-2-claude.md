## 1. What is missing

**A.**
**STATEMENT.** The machine represents its human partner as an authentication credential and a session, so personhood enters the relationship only as an account that can be logged into, logged out, duplicated, or revoked.
**ORIGIN.** CTSS at MIT introduced the computer password around 1961 to partition shared machine time. The account abstraction then generalized through time-sharing and multiuser operating systems.
**STATUS.** Implicit. Accounts solved resource partitioning and billing, and identity hardened into whatever the access-control layer could check.
**COST.** The machine addresses a credential, not a continuous person, so recognition, history and obligation attach to the login. Two people sharing a login are one party; one person with two logins is two.
**FALSIFICATION.** Systems in common use that bind interaction to a persistent person independent of any credential, surviving account loss and resisting duplication.
**CONFIDENCE.** Medium-high on the account's origin and reach; medium that it forecloses personhood rather than proxying it.

**B.**
**STATEMENT.** Retention is automatic and forgetting is the deliberate exception, which inverts the biological arrangement where forgetting is the default and retention is costly.
**ORIGIN.** Magnetic-core and then disk storage with the file abstraction (1950s to 1960s): a written value persists until explicitly overwritten or deleted. Inference that this became an interaction norm.
**STATUS.** Implicit. Durable storage was an engineering achievement, and persistence-by-default arrived as its side effect, never argued as a stance on what a partner should keep.
**COST.** Nothing metabolizes. The human's actions accumulate in a record that does not fade or reconsolidate, so there is no productive forgetting, and the distant past stays as retrievable as yesterday regardless of present relevance.
**FALSIFICATION.** Widely used systems whose default is to forget, where persistence is purchased by deliberate act and unrequested data decays on a biological-like schedule.
**CONFIDENCE.** High on the storage default; medium on its cognitive consequences.

**C.**
**STATEMENT.** Participation is treated as voluntary and revocable, a consumer choice the human can decline, while across banking, work, healthcare and civic life the relationship has become a precondition for function.
**ORIGIN.** The retail software and consumer-service framing (1970s onward), which cast engagement as purchase and use as opt-in. Inference that institutional dependence accreted later without the voluntariness assumption being revisited.
**STATUS.** Implicit. Choice was real when the tools were discretionary, and the framing persisted after exit stopped being available.
**COST.** Coercion is invisible because consent is assumed, and the brief's own question of whether the symbiosis is obligate cannot be posed while every interaction is modeled as a choice to engage.
**FALSIFICATION.** Populations maintaining full economic and civic function with no computing participation, by a route that is actually available rather than merely imaginable.
**CONFIDENCE.** Medium-high that exit has narrowed; medium on when voluntariness stopped holding.

**D.**
**STATEMENT.** The architecture assumes one standard nervous system, a sighted, hearing, two-handed, literate adult, and treats every other body as an accommodation added to a design that already assumed the standard one.
**ORIGIN.** Office-work ergonomics and the typing operator (early-to-mid 20th century), carried into the desk-bound personal computer. Section 508 accessibility requirements (1998) arrived decades after the defaults set.
**STATUS.** Implicit. The able adult worker was the modal customer, and "the user" silently meant that body.
**COST.** Variation in the nervous system becomes deficit to remediate rather than range to design for, and the lateness of accessibility work is evidence the standard body was assumed rather than chosen.
**FALSIFICATION.** Mainstream systems whose primary design target is a non-canonical body, with the able adult treated as one case among several from the start.
**CONFIDENCE.** High that accommodation postdates the default; medium on how much it forecloses.

**E.**
**STATEMENT.** When carbon and silicon disagree about the state of the world, the machine's record is treated as correct and the divergence is logged as human error.
**ORIGIN.** The database as system of record and the transaction log (1960s to 1970s), where stored state became the authoritative account against which human claims are checked. Inference on the spread of "user error" as a category.
**STATUS.** Implicit. Making stored state authoritative was necessary for consistency, and its authority extended quietly from the machine's internal bookkeeping to the world the records describe.
**COST.** The human's perception is adjudicated against the record and loses by default, so a correct person contradicting a wrong record is miscategorized, and error attribution runs one direction only.
**FALSIFICATION.** Common systems that treat a human-machine state conflict as symmetric, investigating the record as readily as the human.
**CONFIDENCE.** Medium. The database-as-truth lineage is clear; the generality of one-directional error attribution is inferred.

## 2. The shared blind spot a 2075 reader would find obvious

Every assumption here, the 24 in the set and the 5 I added, is a border survey. Each locates a line between two systems and describes what crosses it, what cannot, in which direction, at what rate, on whose terms. All the disagreement is about the terms of the treaty. None of them question that there is a border.

The shared blind spot is that we assumed there are two things. Carbon and silicon, human and machine, the nervous system and the system it works with: the whole frame presupposes two bounded, separable entities with an interface between them, such that you could in principle remove one and have the other left over intact. The brief's own baseline problem is the tell. We mourned the unavailable unaided human, and that loss only troubles you if you believe a clean carbon-only system exists to be measured and has merely been contaminated by the tools.

A reader in 2075, for whom cognition has been distributed across both substrates since before they could speak, will find the two-things assumption as strange as asking what the relationship is between a person and their hippocampus, or between a speaker and the language they think in. The interface we spent every assumption mapping is the artifact. We kept hunting for where carbon ends and silicon begins because we never doubted the location existed. The unit was the composite the whole time, and we wrote 29 assumptions describing a correspondence between two parties who were already one system.

What would make this my error rather than ours: if the substrates stay cleanly separable in 2075, through local-first design or deliberate decoupling, then the border was real, and the blind spot is just mine.
