## Voice Rules — Non-Negotiable
- No em-dashes. Ever. Hard ban.
- No colon-fragment constructions ("What it does: this")
- No bullet points in prose
- No "Not X. Y." negation-then-assertion constructions (FATAL)
- No staccato fragment chains (3+ in a row)
- Short declarative sentences. Authoritative, plain. Medical precision without jargon.
- Aphorisms welcome — short memorable lines are a feature, not TED Talk
- First-person physician voice, woven in naturally
- Always run the Critic agent before saving final drafts

Critic protocol: `critic.md` in this folder.
Voice reference: `VOICE-REFERENCE-Foundation2.md` in this folder.
