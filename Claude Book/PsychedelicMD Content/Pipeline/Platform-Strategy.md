# Platform Strategy

## The Core Principle

Lead with the ideas that stand entirely on their own. The brain as outdated software. The difference between treating symptoms and treating causes. What healing actually means. Let the psychedelics connection emerge from the education — never announce it in the title or hook.

---

## YouTube (Primary Platform)

**Why:** YouTube is a search engine. A video titled "Why your brain is running outdated software" surfaces for years to people actively searching for that idea. This is where the long-form educational content lives and compounds over time.

**Format:** 3-8 minutes. Talking head (avatar) with text overlays and occasional B-roll or animated visuals.

**Title approach:** Lead with the brain/medicine/healing angle, not the psychedelics angle.
- "Why Modern Medicine Treats the Wrong Thing" ✓
- "Your Brain Has Never Offered You an Update" ✓
- "Why Psychedelics Cure Depression" ✗ (too direct, too early)

**Cadence:** 1 video per week once pipeline is running.

**Priority content:**
1. The Brain Pitch (3-4 min)
2. Firmware vs. Software / Foundation 2 (4-5 min)
3. We Treat What We Can Fix (3 min)
4. Psychedelics Don't Fix You (3 min)
5. The Update (2 min)

---

## LinkedIn (Secondary Platform)

**Why:** Your professional audience is here. Physicians, researchers, mental health professionals, policy people. Content that would get flagged on other platforms tends to find a more receptive audience on LinkedIn when framed as clinical and professional.

**Format:** 1-3 minutes. Same avatar footage, tighter edit. Native video upload performs better than YouTube links.

**Title approach:** Physician voice first. "As a physician, here is what I think we are getting wrong about healing."

**Cadence:** 2-3 times per week. Mix of short video clips and written posts.

---

## Instagram / TikTok (Supporting)

**Why:** Reach. Different demographic. Good for short, punchy ideas that hook quickly.

**Format:** 60-90 seconds maximum. Hook in the first 3 seconds. Captions always on (most people watch without sound).

**Best content for this format:**
- The Update (60 sec version)
- Single ideas pulled from longer videos
- "Did you know your brain has never offered you an update?" style hooks

**Cadence:** 3-5 short clips per week, mostly repurposed from YouTube content via Opus Clip.

---

## Opus Clip: The Repurposing Tool

Once a YouTube video is published, Opus Clip automatically identifies the most compelling 60-90 second moments and cuts them into short-form clips with captions. One 5-minute YouTube video becomes 3-5 Instagram/TikTok clips with minimal manual effort. This is the efficiency multiplier for the whole operation.

---

## What to Avoid

- Titles or thumbnails that look like drug promotion
- Claims about treating specific conditions without appropriate qualification
- Content that would give regulators or platforms a reason to restrict the channel
- Anything that undermines the physician credibility that makes the whole operation work
