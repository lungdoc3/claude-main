# HeyGen Avatar Setup

## What You Need Before Starting

- HeyGen account (heygen.com) — Creator plan or above for custom avatars
- A recording of yourself speaking: 2-5 minutes minimum, good lighting, neutral background, steady camera, no background noise
- The recording should include natural pauses, varied sentence lengths, and some emotional range — the more expressive the source material, the better the avatar performs

---

## Recording Tips for the Training Video

- Use your phone on a tripod or propped steady — no handheld
- Film in landscape (horizontal)
- Natural light facing you, not behind you
- Plain wall or simple background
- Speak at your natural pace — do not slow down artificially
- Look directly at the lens, not the screen
- Wear what you would wear in the actual videos
- Record for at least 3-5 minutes continuously

---

## Training the Avatar

1. Log into HeyGen
2. Go to Avatars → Create Avatar → Video Avatar
3. Upload your recording
4. HeyGen processes it (usually 24-48 hours)
5. Test with a short script before committing to production

---

## Script Input Best Practices

- Keep sentences shorter than you would write them — the avatar handles short sentences better than long complex ones
- Spell out abbreviations and acronyms phonetically if needed (e.g., "DMT" vs "dee-em-tee")
- Add [pause] markers where you want a natural beat
- Medical and plant medicine terms: test pronunciation first — HeyGen sometimes mispronounces specialty vocabulary
- Maximum recommended script length per video: 600-800 words (roughly 4-5 minutes at natural speaking pace)

---

## Known Limitations

- Highly expressive or emotional delivery is harder to replicate than calm, authoritative delivery — the physician voice actually works in your favor here
- Hand gestures are limited in the avatar — supplementary visuals and text overlays compensate for this
- Pronunciation of "ayahuasca," "psilocybin," "Banisteriopsis caapi" etc. will need to be checked and possibly phonetically respelled in the script
