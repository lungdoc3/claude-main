# PsychedelicMD Content Engine

This project converts book material into short-form video content for public education and book promotion. The goal is to educate — about the brain, about healing, about what is broken in modern medicine — without it reading as a pitch for psychedelics.

---

## Folder Structure

**Video Scripts/**
Adapted scripts from book chapters, formatted for spoken delivery. Each script notes the source chapter, target platform, and estimated runtime.

**Pipeline/**
Documentation for the production workflow: HeyGen avatar setup, Make.com automation, platform strategy, and publishing checklists.

**Published/**
Tracking log for what has been published, where, when, and how it performed.

**Assets/**
Brand notes, visual style guidelines, tone reference, and anything that needs to stay consistent across videos.

---

## The Content Strategy in One Paragraph

The book contains several pieces that are already structured as short educational videos. The approach is to lead with the ideas that stand entirely on their own — the brain as an outdated operating system, the difference between treating symptoms and treating causes, the startup pitch for The Brain — and let the psychedelics connection emerge naturally from the education rather than being announced. The physician's voice is the credential. The ideas are the hook.
