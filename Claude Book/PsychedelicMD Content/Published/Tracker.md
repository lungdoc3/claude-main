## How to Use This Tracker

When a video is published, add a row with:
- A short title
- Which book chapter it came from
- Links to each platform it was published on
- Date published
- Any notes (performance, feedback, follow-up ideas)

This becomes the record of what has been published and where, and makes it easy to spot gaps in the content calendar.
