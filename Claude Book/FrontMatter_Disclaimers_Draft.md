# A Note on Boundaries
## Front Matter — Legal and Medical Disclaimer

---

This book is written for adults who are thinking seriously about psychedelic healing — whether as potential participants, as people who support them, or as clinicians trying to understand a domain their training did not cover. It is not written for any other purpose, and it does not serve any other purpose.

Let me be direct about what it is and what it is not.

---

**This book does not encourage, instruct, or assist anyone in obtaining or using any controlled substance.** The legal status of psilocybin, mescaline, DMT, and related compounds varies by country, state, and jurisdiction, and changes over time. What is legal in one place is a criminal matter in another. Knowing and following the laws where you live and travel is your responsibility, not this book's. Nothing in these pages should be read as guidance on how to circumvent those laws, and nothing is intended as such.

**This book is not medical advice.** It does not establish a physician-patient relationship between the author and any reader. It does not replace consultation with a licensed clinician who knows your history, your medications, and your specific circumstances. The clinical information in Part 4 is offered to expand understanding, not to substitute for individual assessment. If you have a medical condition, take prescription medications, or have a personal or family history of serious psychiatric illness, a qualified healthcare professional is the appropriate source of guidance before any clinical decision, including the decision to pursue psychedelic work.

**This material is intended for adults.** Psychedelic experiences carry real psychological weight. They can be destabilizing for people with certain mental health risks, significant life instability, or vulnerability that may not be fully apparent in advance. The age and psychological readiness of anyone considering this work are serious considerations, not formalities.

**This book does not provide instructions for conducting psychedelic sessions.** The sections on pharmacology, drug interactions, preparation, and facilitation exist as harm-reduction and clinical education material. They are not a protocol. They are not a training program. Reading this book does not qualify anyone to facilitate psychedelic experiences for others.

**This book does not endorse or recommend any specific facilitator, retreat center, or organization.** No commercial relationship of any kind exists between the author and any practitioner or program. The guidance offered here on choosing a facilitator reflects general principles, not endorsement of any specific individual or setting.

---

One more thing. If you or someone in your care is experiencing severe psychological distress, acute confusion, medical symptoms, or feels unsafe — in any context, at any time — seek qualified professional help immediately. Local emergency services exist for exactly this. Use them.

---

*The perspective in this book emerges from time spent at the intersection of western medicine and plant medicine, as a physician, as a practitioner, and as a human being who has taken these questions seriously for a long time. The observations offered here are clinical reflections, not clinical guidelines. They come from experience, not from certainty. Any reader who brings that same spirit of honest inquiry to this material will find what they are looking for.*
