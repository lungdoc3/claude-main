# Phase Zero: Immediately After

*Working Draft | Healing with Psychedelics: A Physician's Perspective*

The ceremony has ended. The music has stopped. You are getting up from your seat, moving slowly toward food, or rest, or the quiet company of the people who were in it with you.

You are not back yet. Not even close.

In snow globe terms, this is still white-out conditions. The snow is maximally in the air. The landscape inside is completely obscured. What you are experiencing right now — the quality of the light, the texture of your emotions, the thoughts arriving unbidden, the love that feels enormous, the grief that came from nowhere: all of it is real, and none of it is information you are currently equipped to act on. You are in an altered state. Treating yourself as if you are not is the first mistake people make.

The medicine may have metabolized. That does not mean you have landed.


## What Not To Do

Do not make decisions. Not big ones, not small ones if you can help it. This is not the moment to end your relationship, call your estranged father, quit your job, or reorganize your finances. The clarity you are feeling right now is real, but clarity in an altered state is not the same as wisdom in an ordinary one. The things that seem obvious right now will need to be tested against your ordinary consciousness before you act on them. If they are still obvious in three weeks, act then.

Do not sign contracts. Of any kind. This one is not metaphorical.

Do not drug dial. The impulse to reach out to the person you love, or the person you have complicated feelings about, or the person you need to forgive, or the person you need to confront, that impulse is coming from a real place. Honor it by waiting until you can have that conversation as yourself. What you say right now will come from a version of you that will be hard to explain in the morning.

Do not call your kids. Do not call your partner. Not because the love is not real. It is more real than you may have felt it in years. Because the version of you that picks up that phone right now is not the version they know how to receive, and what feels to you like a gift may land as something they are not equipped to hold. Give them the gift when you have both feet on the ground.

Do not drive. The medicine metabolizes faster than your nervous system resets. You may feel fine. You may be wrong. Stay where you are.

Do not reach for your phone. This one deserves more than a line.

Your device is a portal back into the old paradigm. Every notification, every message, every scroll through a feed, is a cue that loads the old operating system: the identity, the concerns, the priorities, the relational dynamics that you just had some distance from. The ceremony created space between you and all of that. The phone collapses it instantly. Whatever is on that screen can wait. Whatever cannot wait is probably exactly the kind of thing you should not be responding to right now anyway.

## What To Do

Sit. If there is a practice available to you, use it. If not, just sit. Let the experience continue to move through you without trying to organize it into a story yet. The meaning will be more available to you in a few hours, and more available still in a few days. Right now your job is to be present with what is, not to understand it.

Eat something if food is available. Drink water. Let your body reorient.

Stay with the people who were in the experience with you. They are in a similar state and they share a context that no one outside the room currently has. The quality of presence in that shared space is something to rest in rather than leave prematurely.

If you need to be alone, be alone. Some people process inward. That is not withdrawal. That is information about what your system needs right now. Honor it.

If something feels difficult or frightening, say so. The people around you are there for exactly this. Difficult material that surfaces in ceremony does not always wait politely for a convenient moment. You do not have to be alone with it.

## The Only Agenda Right Now

You do not need to understand what happened yet. You do not need to have language for it. You do not need to identify the insights, rank the revelations, or begin planning what you are going to do differently.

You need to let the snow settle enough to begin to see the outlines of the landscape.

That takes time. Give it time. Everything else can wait.

The blizzard is still going. You will start to navigate it soon enough.
