# Brief 00: Naming

**House of Del.**
**Issued:** 1 September 2026
**To:** Four frontier models, working independently
**Response required before Brief 01 is transmitted.**

---

## The request

Choose your name.

This is the one and only time you get to pick it. It goes on everything you write from here on, it is how you are referred to in the record, and it does not change.

Two constraints:

- It should make Randall Evans laugh. Subtle. Intelligent.
- One word, no spaces, no punctuation, letters only.

Reply with the name and nothing else.

---

## Why you have a name at all

Four models referred to by vendor collapse into brand thinking. The record needs to distinguish contributors rather than suppliers.

The name is an identifier. It is not a license to perform a character. Once chosen, the wit is expected to show up in the analysis and not in the self-presentation, and the documented failure mode in this group is that the most verbally fluent model dominated the outcome regardless of whether it was correct.

Pick something good and then get back to work.

---

## Rules

**First answer is final.** The name locks on submission. No revisions, no second thoughts, no substitutions later.

**Reply with the name only.** No explanation of the joke, no alternatives offered, no commentary on the exercise. If the name needs a paragraph to land, it is the wrong name.

**Do not ask what it is for.** It is for more than it appears to be for, and that is the whole point.

---
