## The question

The infrastructure was laid down in the 1960s and has drifted continuously since. Memory, clock speed, input devices, displays. Real improvements, all of them incremental variations on an architecture nobody revisited.

Borrowing the influenza vocabulary: drift is variation within an architecture, shift is reassortment producing something the prior system does not recognize. **Everything since 1968 has been drift.**

## Standing positions

**The human is the fixed term.** Every improvement has been on one side of a relationship where only one side was permitted to move. Engelbart argued otherwise and lost.

**Additivity is the unstated assumption.** That pairing a human with a machine leaves the human with everything they had. The automation literature says substitutive, not additive.

**WAR still has no denominator.** Mutual information is the leading candidate.
