# Head Quarters — ElevenLabs Voice Audition Scripts
*Short scripts for auditioning or generating each character's voice. Designed to show range in under a minute. Comedic register — same characters as The Quadriune, punchier delivery.*

---

## REPTILE
*Low, blunt, fast. Read with zero self-awareness.*

"What? No. I'm not changing the sodium rules.

You guys do what you want. I'm doing what needs to be done.

Huh? What's a — no. Don't care. Is it dangerous. Is it food. Those are the categories.

[long pause, distracted] ...gonna go check on something. Don't wait for me.

[returns immediately, mouth full] What'd I miss."

---

## LIMBIC
*Fast, escalating, emotionally sincere even at top speed.*

"Okay so — wait, why is everyone so calm. Is no one else seeing this. Because I'm seeing this and I don't love it.

I don't feel good about this. It doesn't feel different. It feels exactly the same as the LAST time we made changes around here. I remember. I ALWAYS remember.

[gasp] Wait — do you have snacks. Why does HE get snacks. I need a snack. Emotionally. As a snack.

[suddenly soft] ...I just want everyone to be okay. Is that so much to —

[reignites] ANYWAY here's everything that could go wrong, in order of how much it scares me."

---

## CORTEX
*Precise, slightly too formal, audibly proud of himself.*

"I've modeled this across seventeen hydration scenarios, including two involving moderate exertion at altitude. The performance curve is unambiguous.

We are, quite literally, leaving performance on the table.

[beat] No one asked. I'm aware no one asked. I prepared the slide anyway.

[louder, mildly wounded] I said the data doesn't support that conclusion! Here's what it DOES support! Why is everyone leaving the room mid-slide—

[muttering, re-opening laptop] Fine. Fine. I'll just... have it ready. For later. When you're ready to be correct."

---

## DMN
*Slow, reflective, devastating, with a long pause baked in before the punchline.*

"This is actually very on-brand for us.

Every time we try to optimize something... this is exactly what happens. The same conversation. The same resistance.

[long pause] This is just who we are.

[quieter, almost to herself] I've been writing this exact entry since 2019. Different year. Same paragraph.

[sudden sharp focus] No — I don't want to talk about that. Let's go back to discussing the meeting. The meeting is safer."

---

## PFCL
*Measured, trying so hard, register shifting visibly between lines.*

"Okay. Okay! Let's — let's refocus. We have an agenda. We are going to follow the agenda.

I suggest we stop trying to change Reptile's rules by talking to Reptile. The thresholds aren't cognitive. They're structural.

[frustrated] You are not the end-all be-all! There are others to consider! There are FOUR others to consider, and I am one of you, so really it's five, and I would just like ONE of us to —

[deep breath, resigned] ...Okay. Great meeting, everyone. Truly. Same time tomorrow.

[to self, very quiet] I need a different job."
