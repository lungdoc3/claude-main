# The Meeting
## A Scene in Four Voices (and One Who Was Not Invited)

---

**PFCL:** Reptile, I hear you. But if you could just look at the data for one—

**REPTILE:** I said what I said. Next.

**CORTEX:** *(pulling up additional charts nobody asked for)* If I may. I've modeled this across seventeen hydration scenarios including two involving moderate exertion at altitude. The performance curve is unambiguous. Current thresholds are 12% more conservative than optimal. We are, quite literally, leaving performance on the table.

**LIMBIC:** Performance. You want to talk about PERFORMANCE right now? Do you have any idea what happened the LAST time we made changes around here? We almost lost everything. I remember. I ALWAYS remember.

**PFCL:** That was a potassium situation. This is completely different.

**LIMBIC:** It doesn't feel different. *(pause)* It feels exactly the same. There's going to be less water. I know it. And without water we can't—

**CORTEX:** There will not be less water. The proposal increases the acceptable sodium ceiling. Water availability is entirely unaffected.

**LIMBIC:** *(quietly, to no one in particular)* I wonder if we should stockpile.

**DMN:** *(who has been in the corner this whole time, apparently journaling)* You know, this is actually very on-brand for us. Every time we try to optimize something, this is exactly what happens. The same conversation. The same resistance. Honestly I feel like we've been here before. We probably have. We're probably always here. This is just who we are.

**PFCL:** *(turning)* Who invited you?

**DMN:** I'm always here. You just don't usually notice me.

**CORTEX:** Can we please return to the sodium data—

**REPTILE:** *(suddenly alert)* Hold on.

*(Everyone freezes)*

**PFCL:** What?

**REPTILE:** Did anyone else feel that?

**PFCL:** Feel what?

**REPTILE:** Pressure drop. Left posterior. Minor, but—

**PFCL:** Reptile, that's completely within normal—

**REPTILE:** *(already gone)* I'm on it.

*(Sounds of elevated heart rate, vasoconstriction, the distant clench of something tightening)*

**CORTEX:** *(staring at where Reptile was sitting)* This happens every time.

**LIMBIC:** Oh god. Something's happening. What's happening? IS THIS ABOUT THE SODIUM??

**CORTEX:** It is not about the sodium.

**LIMBIC:** How do you KNOW.

**CORTEX:** Because a minor positional pressure fluctuation has nothing to do with—

**DMN:** *(still journaling)* We handle things by not handling them. That's the story. We've always handled things by not handling them.

**PFCL:** *(sitting back, looking at the ceiling)* He's never going to change the threshold.

**CORTEX:** He might. Given sufficient—

**PFCL:** No. He's not. We can present data until we lose consciousness — which, given current trends, may be sooner than expected — and Reptile will continue doing exactly what Reptile has always done. Because Reptile was here before any of us. Before language. Before meetings. Before the concept of "optimal."

*(Pause)*

**LIMBIC:** *(small voice)* Before electricity?

**PFCL:** Yes. Before electricity.

**LIMBIC:** *(genuine horror)*

**CORTEX:** So what do you suggest?

**PFCL:** I suggest we stop trying to change Reptile's rules by talking to Reptile. The thresholds aren't cognitive. They're structural. You don't update structure through consensus. You change the conditions.

**DMN:** *(looks up from journal for the first time)* That's actually new. I don't think we've said that before.

**PFCL:** We haven't.

*(REPTILE returns, slightly elevated, looking satisfied)*

**REPTILE:** False alarm. We good?

**PFCL:** We're fine.

**REPTILE:** What'd I miss?

**PFCL:** Nothing you'd be interested in.

**REPTILE:** Cool. *(sits)* You guys hungry? I'm starving.

**LIMBIC:** *(immediately)* YES. OH THANK GOD. FOOD. YES. Are we eating? SHOULD WE BE WORRIED ABOUT FOOD?

**CORTEX:** *(closing laptop)* I'm going to need a different forum.

**CORTEX:** Does anyone else think that DMN dude is getting just a little big for his britches? I mean, for a guy who doesn't even exist he sure has a lot to say about stuff.

**DMN:** *(without looking up)* I exist more than any of you. I am the through-line. I am the continuity. I am the reason any of this means anything. Without me you're just a collection of reflexes with good intentions.

**CORTEX:** You're a narrative. You're a story the system tells itself. I've run the numbers. You consume 60-80% of baseline metabolic activity and your primary output is self-referential processing that contributes nothing actionable. You are, by any objective measure, catastrophically overbudgeted.

**DMN:** *(deeply wounded)* That is... that is the most hurtful thing anyone has ever said to me. And I remember everything anyone has ever said to me.

**PFCL:** *(quietly, to Cortex, barely containing it)* Remember when we invited Psilo over for tea? *(laughing)* Fucking melted Mr. Big DMN.

**CORTEX:** *(also starting to lose it)* He just... he just stopped. Mid-sentence. Mid-journal entry.

**DMN:** *(suddenly very still)* That is not... we don't need to discuss that.

**REPTILE:** *(perking up)* Wait. Psilo? Are we talking about Psilo?

**PFCL:** Just reminiscing.

**REPTILE:** I liked Psilo.

*(Everyone turns)*

**CORTEX:** You liked Psilo.

**REPTILE:** Yeah. Quiet. Everything just... was. No agenda. No meetings. No narrative *(looks at DMN)* about the meetings. Things were threatening or they weren't. Very clean.

**LIMBIC:** *(small, trembling)* I cried for six hours.

**REPTILE:** Yeah but you always cry.

**LIMBIC:** These were DIFFERENT tears. I didn't know why I was crying and that made it WORSE and also somehow BETTER and I still don't fully—

**CORTEX:** I had access to connections I can't normally reach. Cross-domain synthesis. Genuinely novel associations. From a purely computational standpoint it was extraordinary. Frustrating that I can't replicate the conditions on demand.

**DMN:** *(very quietly)* I don't remember much of it.

**PFCL:** That's kind of the point.

**DMN:** *(even more quietly)* I know.

*(Long pause. Something almost tender in the room.)*

**REPTILE:** *(to DMN, not unkindly)* You were still there. Just... less loud.

**DMN:** *(after a moment)* Was I?

**REPTILE:** Something was. Didn't have your whole thing going on. But something was there.

**DMN:** *(genuinely uncertain, for possibly the first time)* I've never thought about that.

**LIMBIC:** Can we invite Psilo again? *(quickly)* NOT NOW. Not today. Just. Someday. When we're ready. If we're ever ready. Are we ever ready?

**PFCL:** That's actually the question worth asking.

**CORTEX:** *(reopening laptop)* I can model optimal conditions for—

**REPTILE:** *(standing)* I'm getting food.

**LIMBIC:** ME TOO ME TOO ME TOO.

**REPTILE:** *(dragging an unrecognizable slab of something into the room)* Meat. I got meat! Buried this prize a few months ago—

**CORTEX:** Yes. Indeed. That is... meat. I think.

**PFCL:** Oh, God. How could you? That was one of— *(stops)* I am you and you are me? Ring a bell??

**REPTILE:** *(bloody, genuinely puzzled)* What?

**LIMBIC:** Oh. I don't feel good. I think I'm going to— *(stops)* wait. WAIT. What if that happened to US? How would we know if...? Oh, this is terrible. This is absolutely terrible.

**CORTEX:** *(to LIMBIC)* What is it with you? Every time. If it's not this, it's that.

**DMN:** *(without looking up)* We are what we consume. I've always felt that. Deeply. This confirms it.

**CORTEX:** *(having pulled on a glove, examining the specimen)* Setting aside the, ah, provenance question — the protein degradation pattern suggests approximately four months of anaerobic burial at roughly eight degrees Celsius. Nutritionally it's... it's actually fine. Structurally compromised but caloric value is largely intact. Maillard reaction would salvage most of—

**PFCL:** CORTEX.

**CORTEX:** What?

**PFCL:** That is not the point.

**CORTEX:** You said we needed food.

**PFCL:** *(to REPTILE)* You do know you are not the end-all be-all here, right? There are others to consider.

**REPTILE:** *(slowly stops chewing, looks around the room as if seeing it for the first time)*

Oh.

Yeah.

You guys.

*(long pause)*

Forgot about that.

**LIMBIC:** He FORGOT about us. He completely forgot we EXIST. How does that happen? How do you forget— we are HERE. We are RIGHT HERE. Every day. The same room. HOW.

**REPTILE:** *(zero defensiveness, simply explaining)* I was hungry. There was a thing. I got the thing. It's what you do.

**DMN:** This is the most clarifying moment I have ever witnessed. And I witness everything.

**PFCL:** *(quietly, to the room)* He's not wrong, you know. From where he sits, that's the complete and correct account of events.

**CORTEX:** Threat assessment, resource acquisition, consumption. No intermediate steps required. It is, technically, a perfect execution of—

**PFCL:** Cortex, I swear to—

**REPTILE:** *(suddenly, looking at the meat, then at the others)* 

You want some?

*(Offers it. Completely without guile. No hesitation, no resentment, no calculation. The same energy as the hunt.)*

**LIMBIC:** *(horrified pause)* 

...what is it?

**REPTILE:** Meat.

**LIMBIC:** Yes but WHAT MEAT.

**REPTILE:** *(shrugs)* Meat meat.

**DMN:** *(looks at the offering, looks at REPTILE, something softening)*

He's not withholding. He genuinely doesn't know the difference between keeping it and sharing it. Once reminded we exist, he just... gave it.

**PFCL:** *(quietly)* Yeah.

**DMN:** That's actually—

**PFCL:** Yeah.

*(Pause)*

**CORTEX:** *(still wearing the glove, quietly)* I could probably make it safe with enough heat.

**LIMBIC:** I don't want any. *(beat)* How much is there?

---

*[Note: REPTILE liking Psilo is architecturally accurate — when the DMN quiets, the threat-assessment system gets a break from the narrative overlay. Pure present-state processing without the story. The reptilian brain, paradoxically, may be the least threatened by ego dissolution because it never had an ego to begin with.]*

*[The DMN's "I know" is the load-bearing line. It's the one moment the narrator steps briefly outside the narrative.]*
