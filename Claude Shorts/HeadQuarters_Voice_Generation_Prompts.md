# Head Quarters — ElevenLabs Voice Design Prompts
*Text-to-voice description prompts for ElevenLabs Voice Design, if generating AI voices rather than casting/cloning. Pair with the audition scripts for testing.*

---

## REPTILE
```
A gruff, low-pitched male voice, blunt and economical, speaks in short clipped bursts with almost no inflection variance. Sounds mildly impatient at all times, not angry -- just efficient. Slightly gravelly texture, like he doesn't talk much and isn't planning to start now. No vocal fry, no uptalk. Think: a bouncer who is also somehow a toddler's idea of a caveman.
```

---

## LIMBIC
```
A warm, breathy female voice that speeds up noticeably when anxious and has a wide emotional range within a single sentence. Slight vocal shake at emotional peaks, quick intakes of breath between rapid clauses. Capable of pivoting from panic to genuine tenderness in under two seconds. Youthful, expressive, a little theatrical -- like someone who feels things at full volume even when whispering.
```

---

## CORTEX
```
A precise, slightly nasal male voice with crisp articulation and a faint air of self-satisfaction. Speaks at a measured, even pace, with deliberate micro-pauses before key numbers or conclusions, as if double-checking himself mid-sentence. Volume increases noticeably when he feels unheard. Reads like a TED-talk voice that hasn't fully adjusted to being interrupted constantly.
```

---

## DMN
```
A soft, low, unhurried female voice with a dreamy, slightly distant quality, as though she's narrating from one room over. Long natural pauses before key statements. Occasional flat, deadpan delivery on devastating lines -- no dramatic emphasis needed because the words do the work. A faint echo-like warmth, like a voice you'd hear in a memory rather than in the room.
```

---

## PFCL
```
A measured, professional female voice with the controlled calm of someone holding it together in real time. Pace shifts visibly with mood -- steady and facilitative, then faster and tighter when frustrated, then slow and flat when resigned, then briefly lighter when hopeful. A worn-thin warmth underneath the exhaustion, like someone who still believes the meeting could work, against all available evidence.
```
