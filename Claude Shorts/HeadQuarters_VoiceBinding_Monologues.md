# Head Quarters — 30-Second Voice-Binding Monologues
*In-character monologues for the Kling element voice-binding step, once character art exists. Each runs roughly 30 seconds at a natural pace. Comedic register throughout — these establish the voice + face binding for each Pixar-style character.*

---

## REPTILE

"Okay, here's the thing nobody wants to hear: I don't care about your feelings, and I don't care about your spreadsheet, and I definitely don't care about whatever CORTEX is presenting right now. You're alive. You're fed. You're warm enough. That's the whole job. That's been the whole job for three hundred million years and it's still the whole job. Everyone else in this building is doing extra credit. I'm doing the assignment. Also — is that food? That's food. I'm taking that. Meeting's over."

---

## LIMBIC

"Okay, so, first of all — I love you, I love everyone in this room, that's not in question. But I need everyone to understand that I have been tracking every single bad thing that has ever happened to us, in order, with feelings attached, and I will not be told to 'relax' by someone who does not have my filing system. Is it scary in here right now? A little. Is it always a little scary? Yes. That's not pessimism, that's preparedness. Anyway. Does anyone want a hug or are we all just going to stand here being fine."

---

## CORTEX

"I'd like to begin by noting that I prepared a slide for this, which no one requested, which I am presenting anyway, because the data doesn't care about your meeting agenda. I've run the numbers. Several times. From multiple angles. The conclusion is the same every time, which is either deeply reassuring or extremely boring depending on who you ask, and I already know who's going to say 'boring.' I'm looking at you. Anyway — decimal places matter, nuance matters, and yes, I will say all of this again in fifteen minutes whether you've finished processing it or not."

---

## DMN

"You'll notice I've been quiet. That's not nothing — that's information. I've been compiling, as I always am, the story of how we got here, which is, conveniently, also the story of who we are, which is, even more conveniently, a story I happen to be the only one qualified to tell. Is that a conflict of interest? Possibly. I've written several entries about that exact question. Anyway, this is all very on-brand for us, historically speaking — and before you ask, no, I will not be elaborating on what happened the last time it got quiet in here."

---

## PFCL

"Alright, everyone — thank you for being here, I know we're all very busy being various other things. I just want to say, gently, that I have prepared an agenda, and that the agenda has goals, and that historically the goals and the meeting have had what I'd call a 'complicated relationship.' That's fine. That's fine! We grow. Today, I would like us to leave this room having made one decision, together, as a system, without anyone leaving for a snack or crying into a blanket or presenting a seventeen-scenario slide deck. ...Does that feel like a lot to ask? It's starting to feel like a lot to ask."
