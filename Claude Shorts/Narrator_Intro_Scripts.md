# The Quadriune — Narrator Intro Scripts
*Off-screen voiceover, plays before each character's own locked line. Written for a first-time viewer who has never heard of the quadriune brain model.*

---

## REPTILE

Three hundred million years before anyone thought to ask what a self even was, this part of you was already running the show. The brainstem doesn't reason and it doesn't wait for permission. It just keeps you breathing, your heart beating, and your body fed.

**Meet REPTILE.**

*(cut to locked clip: "What? No. I'm not changing the sodium rules. You guys do what you want. I'm doing what needs to be done.")*

---

## LIMBIC

Underneath every gut feeling you've ever had is a system built to keep you safe by feeling everything first and asking questions later. It remembers every threat, every loss, every time someone you loved walked away.

**Meet LIMBIC.**

*(cut to locked clip)*

---

## CORTEX

Somewhere in your skull is a part of you that runs the numbers, weighs the options, and can hold an idea in mind without panicking about it. It's the newest part of the brain, evolutionarily speaking, and it knows it.

**Meet CORTEX.**

*(cut to locked clip: "Let's be precise. The data doesn't support that conclusion. Here's what it does.")*

---

## DMN

Even when you're doing nothing, some part of you is still working, stitching your past, your future, and the story of who you are into something that feels like a self. It rarely stops talking, even to itself.

**Meet DMN.**

*(cut to locked clip)*

---

## PFCL

Somebody has to coordinate all of this. Weigh the long game against the urge of the moment. Keep the others from running the whole show. That job belongs to the prefrontal cortex, and it is, by a wide margin, the most tired part of you.

**Meet PFCL.**

*(cut to locked clip: "I suggest we stop trying to change Reptile's rules by talking to Reptile. The thresholds aren't cognitive. They're structural.")*
