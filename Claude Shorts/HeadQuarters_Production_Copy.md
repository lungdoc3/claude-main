# Head Quarters — Production Copy
*Quick-reference sheet for art, voice, and animation work. One block per character: name, role, pitch, visual notes, voice notes, accent color.*

---

## REPTILE — The Brainstem
**One-line pitch:** The ancient one who keeps you alive and could not care less what you think about it.
**Accent color:** `#8BC34A`

**Visual notes:** Squat, low, built like a fire hydrant. Warm leather-mitt skin texture, not literally scaled. Sleeveless hide vest, snack in pocket, a couple of proud scars, blunt teeth, always-scanning eyes. Moves in bursts — motionless, then gone.

**Voice notes:** Short. Declarative. Fast rhythm, not hostile — efficient. Doesn't do abstractions. Says "huh?" a lot, genuinely confused why anyone needs more than four words.

---

## LIMBIC — The Limbic System
**One-line pitch:** Feels everything first, asks questions never, loves you so much it's a structural problem.
**Accent color:** `#EC407A`

**Visual notes:** Round, soft, plush-toy proportions. Saucer eyes, hair-trigger eyebrows. Oversized cardigan-poncho, static hair, tote bag of mismatched "emergency" items. Visibly inflates when alarmed.

**Voice notes:** Rapid, associative, escalates mid-sentence. Interrupts her own thought with a worse one. Stacks questions without waiting for answers. Warm underneath the panic.

---

## CORTEX — The Neocortex
**One-line pitch:** Has the data. Has always had the data. Will show you the data whether or not this is the moment for data.
**Accent color:** `#29B6F6`

**Visual notes:** Tall, reedy, all elbows. Oversized lab coat, glasses sliding down, second pair parked on forehead. Floating charts when excited. Comically huge cold coffee mug.

**Voice notes:** Precise, slightly too formal for the room. Extra decimal places. Pauses before answering — not for drama, for accuracy. Gets louder when ignored.

---

## DMN — The Default Mode Network
**One-line pitch:** Narrates the whole story, including the parts that already happened, including this sentence.
**Accent color:** `#D8A657`

**Visual notes:** Wispy, slightly translucent, drifts more than walks. Oversized cardigan-shawl, too-big beret, journal always open. Orbited by floating sticky notes and photos.

**Voice notes:** Reflective, self-referential, talks about the group rather than to it. Long pause before speaking. Delivers gut-punch lines in a book-review tone.

---

## PFCL — The Prefrontal Cortex
**One-line pitch:** Trying to run the meeting. The meeting is not working. Has had one coffee, four hours ago.
**Accent color:** `#FFA726`

**Visual notes:** Rumpled business-casual, sleeves pushed up, reading glasses on a chain. Whiteboard marker like a sword, dying stress ball. Big "explaining to a toddler" hand gestures. One visible stress-streak.

**Voice notes:** Measured, trying to be fair, shifts register fast — facilitating, frustrated, resigned, briefly hopeful. Says "okay, let's refocus" and means it every single time.
