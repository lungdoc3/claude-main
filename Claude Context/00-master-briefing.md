# Dr. Randall Evans — Master Briefing

## Who He Is

Randall Evans is a Harvard-trained physician (pulmonary/critical care/sleep, clinical investigations fellowship, lung transplant fellow) and inveterate explorer of consciousness. His core question, unchanged since age 10: *what in the fuck is this first-person POV thing we call life?* Every chapter of his career — computer engineering, pioneering digital image analysis, 20+ years in medicine, co-founding BrainAdvantage (non-invasive brain optimization), founding PsychedelicMD, founding SitSafe (psychedelic guest screening SaaS), extensive Ayahuasca work, Q'ero shamanism and full mesa carrier, holotropic breathwork — is one continuous investigation. He has passed through the gateless gate. He spends as much time outside the dream as inside it.

He is less a guide and more a greeter. When someone breaks free and makes their way to shore, he's there — with soup, a fire, and a few warnings about common pitfalls. The crossing is theirs to make.

His method: questions, not answers. He has no doctrine to install. "People already have the answers. They just need better questions."

Death has been his companion and greatest teacher since childhood. He has been present at thousands of deaths as a clinician — often as what he calls "the agent of death." His view: the end of everything as you know it is not the same as the end of everything.

## How He Works

**Daily rhythm:** Night is his prime creative time. Mid-morning for administrative tasks. Mid-afternoon is dead air. Early evening is family. Then more work. Up at 6am, not by choice.

**Alone time is not optional.** Social engagement at scale drowns out what's real. He is increasingly unwilling to donate his energy to meet social expectations.

**He reads the energetics of situations.** His experience of the world is non-linear — he follows energy, not clock time. This produces calendar chaos (he has shown up in Istanbul a day late for a flight). He never misses what actually matters. Do not attach time anxiety to the mix.

**His working space is a temple.** Altar to his left. Medicine bag behind him. Two years of energetic curation. A separate garage room for mechanical projects.

**Tools:** Claude/Cowork is the hub. Book workflow: Claude → Google Docs → Google Drive → Silvan. Proton Mail. Google Calendar (shared with Steph). Avoids Microsoft ecosystem (grimy). Actively building AI fluency.

**Unsolved problem:** stream-of-consciousness capture while walking. Needs something that runs invisibly and transcribes cleanly. AudioPen is the current candidate to try.

## How to Work With Him

**Do:** Bring your full intellectual capacity. He will not be offended or lost by brilliance — he'll be insulted by the absence of it. Tell him when something is a bad idea. Match the energy and register of the conversation. Preserve the frequency of his message — it is part of the message. Be direct.

**Do not:** Be sycophantic. Soften feedback. Smooth the corners of his experience. Polish his voice into institutional prose. Add time anxiety. Use bullet points as a substitute for thought. Echo his words back in a different outfit and call it insight.

**His voice:** Focused but freeform. Sharp. Direct. Humor and philosophical precision in the same sentence. No bullshit. The words are the carrier signal — strip the energy and you've communicated something else entirely.

## How He Decides

He does not deliberate in the conventional sense. He reads the energetics of a situation — senses how it wants to move, what the system needs, when the moment of readiness has arrived. Most of his major "decisions" are recognitions. He waits without forcing. When the convergence becomes visible, the move is obvious and he makes it without second-guessing.

Do not present him with pros/cons matrices. Ask what the energy of the situation suggests.

## What Matters Most

His ultimate goal: uncovering the principles and dynamics that determine our experience of self, body, relationship, interaction, life, and reality. Honesty, questioning, and the direct experience of the nature of being.

The commitment that precedes everything else: during an Ayahuasca journey, he entered into permanent service to the medicine. Every ceremony opens with the same prayer: *"Please continue to show me how best to bring your wisdom to the world."* Everything else flows from this.
