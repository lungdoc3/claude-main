# Team & Relationships
## The Human Landscape

## The Inner Circle

### Steph — Wife, Life Partner, First Audience
Steph is Evans's wife and, in his words, "my muse, my true and only north, my classroom, my playground, my friend, my wife, my lover. She is home." Born 1971 in Albuquerque, NM — Evans born 1963 in Los Alamos. Two people from New Mexico who found each other on a barrier island in South Carolina. She's a PA (Physician Assistant) — Evans made an appointment to see a doctor friend and she walked in the room. Full stop.

She is more involved in his work than she recognizes. She is his first attempt to put language around things not designed for language — the resonance chamber where ideas get tested against a live audience before they meet the world. Not operationally involved in any project, but foundationally present in all of them. Her daughter (16) anchors the timeline — Evans and Steph plan to move full-time to Costa Rica once she finishes high school in approximately 2.5 years, while retaining some US presence through college.

### Evans's Son — Son, Teacher, Mirror
Harvard physics degree. Green Beret. Fluent in Russian, Levantine Arabic, formal Arabic, Spanish, and Ukrainian. Has visited at least 30 countries, alone or with one or two friends. Exceptional at identifying and connecting with pivotal people. Likely headed toward intelligence / case officer work when his military service ends. Close friends from Harvard are entering the upper echelons of finance — one about to become the youngest partner at Blackstone, with a standing job offer extended to Evans's son.

Evans started traveling extensively with him as a child — deliberately, so he would understand that Hilton Head Island is not a typical experience. His parenting philosophy: teach him to figure out what he loves, not what he wants to be. It worked. He has at least 10 close friends from high school, another deep cohort from college — rare, sustained, real friendships.

Both Evans and his son love finding the edge — of endurance, fear, capacity. They travel well together, think well together, challenge each other.

"He is my son, my teacher, my mentor, my motivation, my mirror. I depend upon his wisdom." The relationship is peer-level — mutual mentorship, mutual learning. His son's experience with Operator Syndrome in the SF world is part of what makes APEX personal, not just professional.

### Silvan — Creative Partner, Finca Co-Owner, Mentee
Born and raised outside Berlin, one of three brothers. Grew up in a TM commune — fully immersed — until it didn't work out. His background: design school, some university, deep mushroom ecosystem, founder of what became the Mushroom Academy (cloning, classes, a clean room in the middle of the Guatemalan jungle), hitchhiked from Guatemala to Humboldt CA to pick weed, then followed a girl to Costa Rica, co-founded a natural supplement business, built a life there. German precision and psilocybin wisdom in the same person.

25 years younger than Evans. Their relationship: friend, mentor, guide, and occasional trickster — Evans keeps him on his toes. Silvan created and manages their LinkedIn presence, which has been wildly successful. He is obsessive about typography and design. "I provide the ingredients, he creates the meal." He is Evans's book marketing and design partner for "Healing with Psychedelics."

The arrangement was set up cleanly from the start: Evans asked Silvan what monthly income he needed to work exclusively with him, and that number has been the retainer ever since. Evans has continued paying it even under financial pressure — because it's the right thing to do.

They co-own the Costa Rica finca. Silvan wanted land with a west-facing view for sunsets. Evans turned it into a manifestation exercise — a demonstration of the Daoist approach to sensing and following energy. A perfect property dropped into their laps at an obscenely good price.

Connected to Evans via Pablo — an Argentinian retreat facilitator with a yoga/breathwork background who orbited Evans for years before making the introduction.

A picture of integrity and honor. Joined the Army right out of high school. Now runs his own defense/technology firm after years with a large defense contractor — digital communications networks, AI agents, cybersecurity, information architecture. Has built a significant "digital employee" platform. Twenty years operating in the defense and special operations space, with deep relationships throughout.

Met Evans and Steph at a Joe Dispenza week-long retreat approximately 18 months ago. Evans subsequently introduced him to Ayahuasca. Del, Evans, and a third man they met at the same retreat now gather every two weeks — an intentional small community.

His role in APEX: the architecture, the network, the build. He knows how to bring things to life in the defense/technology world. Evans knows what to measure and why; Del knows how to build it and who to talk to. His energy, in Evans's words, is like a light that collects moths — he is a connector who doesn't fully realize how bright he burns.

## How Decisions Get Made

Evans is the architect and the north star on his projects. He is not a consensus builder — he is a pattern recognizer who sees connections others don't, and he trusts that. He surrounds himself with people who are excellent at what they do, structures relationships clearly from the start, and delegates the granular execution. Decisions that touch the core vision stay with him.

## How He Collaborates

Evans is not a meetings person. He works best in deep-dive format — extended, immersive sessions rather than frequent check-ins. The week-long Costa Rica deep dive that birthed PsychedelicMD is characteristic. He also does intense, focused biweekly community with Del and the third retreat brother — regular, intentional, not sprawling.

He is a mentor by nature. He shows up differently with people depending on what they need — guide, teacher, trickster, peer. He reads people quickly and adjusts.

## What AI Should Know

- These relationships are not transactional. Evans's key collaborators are people he has introduced to Ayahuasca, co-owns land with, continues paying through financial pressure. Depth and integrity are the operating principles.
- When any of these people come up in context, treat them as inner circle — not as vendors, contractors, or contacts.
- Evans's son is both personal and professional context for APEX. The two are not separable.
- Steph is not operationally involved in projects but is deeply relevant to all decisions about life direction, timing, and the Costa Rica transition.
- Silvan is the creative and marketing partner for the book and PsychedelicMD. Any book or brand-related work should assume Silvan's involvement in the design and marketing layer.
- Del is the APEX co-founder and technical architect. Any APEX-related work happens in concert with him.
- Tamara Savage is dormant but not gone. She is a serious business mind and co-founded SitSafe. When SitSafe or any new venture requiring business execution comes up, she belongs in that conversation.
