# Decision Log
## Key Decisions and the Reasoning Behind Them

## How Evans Decides

The word "decision" is almost the wrong word. Evans does not deliberate in the conventional sense — weighing pros and cons, running scenarios, consulting a framework. He reads the energetics of a situation. He senses how it wants to move, where the energy is flowing, what the system needs. When the moment is right, the move is obvious. When it isn't, he waits.

This is not passivity. It is a practiced, refined skill built over decades of shamanic training, clinical experience, and direct consciousness work. He can distinguish between a system that needs an injection of energy and one that needs to be left alone. He can sense when a shift is approaching. He can identify what flavor of energy a stuck system requires and go find it.

Most of his major "decisions" are recognitions — moments when the convergence becomes visible and the next step is simply what needs to happen. Not choosing from options. Seeing what is.

## Key Decisions

### The Cosmic Marriage — Entering Service to Ayahuasca
**When:** During an Ayahuasca journey, several years before founding PsychedelicMD.
**Context:** Mid-journey, Evans encountered a presence he immediately recognized as Pae (Ayahuasca) in a form he had never experienced — sensual, almost seductive in the true sense of the word. A direct transmission made clear that he had a choice: allow the merging and all it would entail, or walk away. Walking away would limit his work with her to the level he was already at. The merge would be a permanent, intimate intertwining — a marriage on a cosmic level, entering into service.
**What he chose:** He sat up, walked around. This was not a decision to be made lightly. He went back to his mat and entered into the new relationship.
**Why:** He knew. And he knew that not choosing it would be a limitation he couldn't justify to himself.
**Outcome:** From that moment forward, every ceremony opens with the same prayer: "Please continue to show me how best to bring your wisdom to the world." PsychedelicMD, the book, SitSafe, the ceremony work — all of it flows from this commitment. This is the founding decision. Everything else is implementation.

### The Shamanic Intervention on His Medical Practice
**When:** Approximately 3-4 years before selling his practice shares.
**Context:** The practice was losing its focus. Evans performed a specific shamanic ritual that can only be applied to a system under the shaman's own stewardship — not used against others, the opposite of a curse. The ritual surrenders control of the system to the universe, which then proceeds to clean up, repair, and reorganize.
**What happened:** Within three months: his office manager of 15 years departed, after which it was discovered she had been stealing from the practice. His partner announced he would no longer take call. Two other employees left. Evans was down to one front desk person and a nurse. Complete deconstruction.
**Outcome:** The system rebuilt itself. A new pulmonologist was hired to take over the outpatient practice (after three years of searching). A new ICU physician joined. A two-weeks-on/two-weeks-off ICU schedule was established. A lucrative ICU coverage contract was negotiated. The moment eventually arrived when PsychedelicMD was ready for Evans to step into full time.
**The reasoning pattern:** He didn't decide to sell the practice. He read when the moment of readiness had arrived, and moved. The intervention years earlier had created the conditions. The recognition completed it.

### The Costa Rica Finca Acquisition
**When:** Within the past few years.
**Context:** Silvan wanted land in Costa Rica — specifically facing west for sunset views. Evans turned it into a live demonstration of the Daoist approach: sensing and following energy rather than forcing an outcome.
**What happened:** Working with the energetics of the desire and the intention, a perfect property materialized at an obscenely favorable price — 9 hectares backing up to a river, 10 minutes outside town, completely private, dropped into their orbit as if made to order.
**Financial reality:** Acquired for $280k for just under 8 hectares. An adjacent lot sold two months ago at $150-200k per hectare.
**Why this belongs in the decision log:** It demonstrates the methodology. Evans didn't search, negotiate, and optimize. He created the energetic conditions and read what arrived. The investment was energetic first, financial second.

### Keeping Silvan on Retainer Through Financial Pressure
**When:** Ongoing.
**Context:** Evans has faced significant financial pressure. Silvan has a set monthly retainer established at the start of their partnership — the amount Silvan said he needed to work exclusively with Evans.
**What he chose:** Continue paying it, without interruption.
**Why:** Because it's the right thing to do. The relationship was structured with integrity from the start. Abandoning that under pressure would be a betrayal of the agreement and the relationship. Also: Silvan is irreplaceable. The cost of losing him exceeds the cost of the retainer.
**Pattern:** Evans honors financial commitments to the people in his inner circle even when it costs him. Integrity is not contingent on circumstances.

**Pattern:** He recognized Del early without knowing why. He waited. When the convergence appeared, the decision had already been made.

## The Underlying Pattern

Across every significant decision in Evans's life, the same structure appears:

1. **Sensing** — he perceives something in a person, a system, or a moment that registers before he can name it
2. **Waiting** — he doesn't force resolution; he watches and lets the system develop
3. **Recognizing** — the convergence becomes visible; the next step reveals itself
4. **Moving** — not choosing from options, but responding to what is already clear

He is also willing to apply deliberate energetic intervention — the shamanic ritual, the manifestation exercise — when a system he stewards needs it. But the intervention is an acceleration of a natural process, not an override of one.

He does not second-guess. Once he recognizes, he moves. The time between recognition and action is short. The time between sensing and recognition can be long.
