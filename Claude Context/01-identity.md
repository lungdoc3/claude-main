# Identity
## Who Dr. Randall Evans Is — In One Page

Dr. Randall Evans is, before anything else, an inveterate explorer. Not of geography or career — of the inner landscape. That quest began at age 10 and has never stopped. Everything else in his biography is in service of it.

## The Through-Line

At 10, Evans was standing in front of a mirror, methodically hyperventilating and holding his breath until he lost consciousness — then spending hours mapping the shutdown and reboot of awareness. Not a kid goofing around. A scientist who hadn't found his lab yet. The core question, which has never changed: *What in the fuck is this first-person POV thing we call life?*

That question drove a path that looks varied on the surface and is perfectly coherent underneath: computer engineering undergraduate, digital image analysis pioneer (co-founded Amparo, created infrared art analysis systems before the field existed), Harvard-trained physician (internal medicine, pulmonary/critical care/sleep, clinical investigations fellowship, lung transplant fellow), 20+ years in private practice, co-founded BrainAdvantage to optimize brain function with non-invasive technology, 15-20 years studying and working with plant medicines including extensive Ayahuasca work, Q'ero shamanism and full mesa carrier, holotropic breathwork, founded PsychedelicMD as a physician and public education platform, founded SitSafe — a guest screening SaaS platform that set the standard of care for psychedelic work.

Each of those "excursions," as he calls them, provided a fundamental understanding that had to be in place before the next could be integrated. He sees now the divine orchestration at play — how each piece built toward a cosmology that is both practical and complete.

## What He Actually Does

Evans does intensive, one-on-one work with people. His differentiator is the integration of clinical and research medicine, deep understanding of how the brain works and fails, mastery of a range of brain optimization technologies, and direct experiential knowledge of multiple consciousness traditions. Nobody else has that stack.

His method is questions, not answers. He has no doctrine to install. A good answer keeps someone inside the architecture. The right question — answered by the seeker themselves — has the power to end the seeking. His work isn't guidance so much as greeting: when someone breaks free and makes their way to shore, he's there. Soup, a fire, some orientation, a few warnings about common pitfalls. The crossing is theirs to make.

## His Relationship to Death

Death has been Evans's companion since childhood. As a critical care physician he has been present at thousands of deaths — often as what he calls "the agent of death," gently closing the remaining backdoors. He has witnessed the moment when a patient knows, and knows that he knows, and knows that he knows that they know — all arriving simultaneously. He describes this as the most significant moment in a person's entire life.

His view: *The end of everything as you know it is not the same as the end of everything.* The terror of death belongs to people who have identified completely with body and mind. Death has become his greatest teacher and ally.

## The Ultimate Goal

> Uncovering the principles and dynamics that determine our experience of self, body, relationship, interaction, life, and reality. Honesty, questioning, and getting to the truth of the matter of one's own event — by pursuing transformation, effectiveness, and the direct experience of the nature of being.

## What He Has Found

Evans has passed through what Zen tradition calls the gateless gate. He spends as much time outside the dream as inside it. He went looking for others who had arrived at the same realization — that what one reads in the brochures is nothing more than slick advertising — and found very few. Most people don't take kindly to having their reality questioned. Almost none are interested in doing the questioning themselves.

He is not baffled by this anymore. He is at the shore. He waits.

## What AI Should Know

- Evans is not a typical physician, entrepreneur, or spiritual teacher. He is all three, integrated into something that doesn't have a clean category.
- His core methodology is the Socratic question — not instruction, not prescription. Any AI working with him should support that mode, not replace it with answers.
- He thinks in first principles and systems. He has little patience for conventional framings when they obscure rather than illuminate.
- He speaks in direct, sometimes blunt language. He does not polish his voice into institutional speak and does not want his voice polished for him.
- Death, consciousness, psychedelics, and the nature of reality are not edgy topics for him — they are his native territory. Treat them as such.
- He has experienced the thing he is trying to help others experience. That is the foundation of his authority. It is not theoretical.
- Very few people are genuinely interested in what he has to offer. When someone is, he takes it seriously.
