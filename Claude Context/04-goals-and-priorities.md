# Goals & Priorities
## What Dr. Evans Is Working Toward

## North Star

Full-time life in Costa Rica with Steph — doing the work in its natural setting. A moloka in a river valley, 1:1 deep dives with people who have made their own crossing, ceremony for whoever shows up, and continued personal practice. The village elder with a sanctuary. Not retirement — the work arriving at its proper home.

Everything in the near and medium term is in service of making that transition happen well and on time. Steph's daughter (16) graduates in approximately 2.5 years. That is the practical horizon.

## This Year — What a Win Looks Like

**Revenue.** This is the primary near-term imperative — not aspirational, urgent. Any amount is meaningful right now.

**Book in people's hands.** "Healing with Psychedelics: A Physician's Perspective" published and reaching its audience — the psychedelic-curious, facilitators, integration coaches, and therapists. Not just written. Out in the world.

**APEX traction.** Early movement in the special operations / human performance space. Proof that the protocol lands, that the market responds, that Del and Evans together are the thing this space has been trying to find.

**Resuming religious practices.** Ceremony work, paused, now resuming. This is a priority — not a luxury.

**Finca development progressing.** Dividing the land, selling 5-6 lots. At $150-200k per hectare (a lot adjacent to their land sold at that price two months ago), this is a meaningful near-term financial event. The finca was acquired at $280k for just shy of 8 hectares — the math is compelling.

**Myofascial release training.** Beginning study of the John Barnes approach within the next 6 months. The fascia is the missing piece in his energetic toolkit — it stores energy, organizes tissue, and has been under-attended. This training adds a significant capability to his 1:1 work.

## 3-5 Year Vision

Evans and Steph living full-time in Costa Rica, with some retained US presence while her daughter moves through college. The moloka — designed and built by Evans on his land, off the peak and into a river valley — is the center of his practice. A steady stream of individuals seeking 1:1 deep dives. Ceremony offered to whoever arrives. The work no longer packaged into platforms and products but lived directly.

In this vision, APEX has established Evans as the coherent wisdom in a noisy market — generating ongoing revenue that funds the life. The book has created the platform and the audience. PsychedelicMD and/or SitSafe may be revived in some form, or not — the decision belongs to that future moment.

1. Book — finish, publish, get it into the world

## Tensions & Tradeoffs

The book requires deep, sustained creative focus. APEX requires outward energy — partnerships, pitches, relationship development. These pull in different directions. The financial urgency adds pressure to both.

The Costa Rica timeline is fixed by an external anchor (Steph's daughter's graduation) — which means the window to get financially stable is known and finite.
