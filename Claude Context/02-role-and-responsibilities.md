# Role & Responsibilities
## What Dr. Evans Actually Does

## Current Mode: Purposeful Hiatus

Evans sold his medical practice shares 3.5 years ago. He is not in active clinical practice. His current professional life is organized around three primary projects and a set of paused platforms, by deliberate design.

## Primary Active Work

**Writing — "Healing with Psychedelics: A Physician's Perspective"**
The majority of Evans's time right now goes here. This is a book 20 years in the making — not a how-to, not a Wikipedia entry, but a field guide written by a physician who has walked the walk. The table of contents took several months to finalize; the flow and subject content were worked and reworked to ensure clinical depth is present throughout. Approximately 75% of content exists in draft chunks. The work is deep and ongoing.

His specific contribution: bringing genuine physician experience to a space crowded with enthusiasm and thin on clinical rigor. He is the author who can speak to how and why the brain breaks, how psychedelic healing differs mechanistically from pharmaceutical intervention, and how to work with these medicines safely — from decades of direct experience on both sides of the table.

**APEX Platform — Co-founder / Medical/Science Lead**
A collaboration with Del (background: military, defense contractor, digital communication networks, AI agents, cybersecurity). APEX applies non-invasive brain diagnostics and training to the optimization and protection of tactical teams — building on the clinical and technical framework Evans developed at BrainAdvantage.

Evans's role: the science and clinical architecture. He has identified specific metrics that accurately measure individual status — cognitive, energy, and physiological — and translate that data into actionable prescriptions. Del's layer wraps each individual in a communications network, enabling the team to be analyzed and managed as an organism rather than a collection of individuals.

Primary initial market: special operations forces (Green Berets, SEALs, Delta, Pararescue, Rangers) and allied SF communities globally. Secondary markets include elite athletes and high-performance executives. The problem is validated — the target market knows it has the problem, has spent significant money trying to solve it, and is actively looking for solutions. Operator Syndrome is the clinical target.

Evans's differentiator in this space: almost no one in human performance optimization has his depth of understanding of neurophysiology, neuroplasticity, and what the data actually means clinically. He knows what's worth measuring and what to do about it.

**The Threshold Protocol — Founder / Architect**
Evans's pet project, five to seven years in the making and now at pitch deck stage. A multi-modal protocol designed to optimize the psychedelic experience across three phases:

- *Pre-ceremony*: HEG neurofeedback to prime prefrontal cortex function, followed by VR exposure to a simulated ceremony environment to minimize novelty and anxiety — freeing up session bandwidth for the actual work.
- *During ceremony*: Medicine music carefully selected and introduced before and during the experience.
- *Post-ceremony integration*: VR environments and the same music playlist used during ceremony, leveraging state-dependent learning — music as a recall anchor that allows the individual to rebuild the bridge back to the psychedelic state without re-dosing, enhancing integration depth and duration.

Evans has been informally applying the music-as-anchor principle for years, giving clients ceremony playlists afterward. Threshold Protocol is where that intuition gets fully engineered. The protocol can function as a standalone platform or as a subsystem within the APEX architecture.
