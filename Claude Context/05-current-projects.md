### The Threshold Protocol
- **Category:** Entrepreneurial / Psychedelic Medicine / Health Technology
- **Status:** Pitch deck stage — concept mature, architecture defined
- **What it is:** A multi-modal protocol to optimize the psychedelic experience across three phases. Pre-ceremony: HEG neurofeedback to prime prefrontal cortex function, plus VR exposure to a simulated ceremony environment to reduce novelty and anxiety. During ceremony: structured medicine music protocol. Post-ceremony: VR-assisted integration plus the same music playlist used during ceremony, leveraging state-dependent learning — music as a recall anchor to partially recreate the psychedelic state and deepen integration without re-dosing. Evans has been informally applying the music-as-anchor principle for years with his own clients. This is the formal architecture.
- **My role:** Founder and architect. This is his pet project — 5-7 years of thinking behind it.
- **Current focus:** Pitch deck development. Can function as a standalone platform or as a subsystem within APEX.
