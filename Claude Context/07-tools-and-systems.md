# Tools & Systems
## How Evans Gets Work Done

## The Honest Overview

The toolkit is lean and in transition. Evans has been actively decluttering — weaning off Word, partially cleaning out Notes, stepping away from tools that create more friction than they remove. The current workflow is more streamlined than it's been, and the direction is toward further simplification. Overall workflow and file destinations need continued de-cluttering — acknowledged and in progress.

The core observation: over the past six months, this AI environment (Claude/Cowork) has become the hub. And something unexpected happened in the process — it has become a tool for rebuilding processes of thought, organization, and relationships. That's not a productivity observation. That's a structural shift.

## Writing Workflow

**The current stack for the book:**
1. **Visual Studio Code** — early thought process, rough drafting, getting ideas out
2. **Claude/Cowork** — refinement, development of ideas, working through structure
3. **Google Docs** — final home for finished pieces; shared with Silvan for book collaboration
4. **Google Drive** — folder structure for completed content

The Table of Contents lives across 4 Google Docs — the skeleton. Content is being assembled as clay pieces placed generally where they belong on the skeleton, then rearranged, edited, smoothed, and eventually refined before it goes "in the kiln." That's the actual working metaphor, not a cleaned-up version.

**What's been retired:** Microsoft Word — largely weaned off. Helped declutter the document collection significantly.

## Calendar & Scheduling

A known friction point, and an important one to understand correctly.

Evans's experience of the world is non-linear — he follows energetics, not clock time. This is not an organizational failing; it is a direct consequence of how his cognition operates after stabilizing on the other side of the gateless gate. What once required no effort (tracking dates, linear time) now requires deliberate attention because it is no longer his native operating mode. The result: missed flights, hotel reservation chaos, showing up in Istanbul a day late. This is real and has to be accounted for.

**What he uses:**
- **Google Calendar** — primary. Shared with Steph for family coordination.
- **iPhone Calendar (default)** — long-standing, since the PalmPilot era
- **Proton Calendar** — synced with Proton email. Exists, used, reasons unclear even to him.
- **Phone alarms** — the actual reliability layer. Set for anything that cannot be missed. "Like I can't miss my pill kinda energy."

**What to understand:** The calendar chaos doesn't reflect inner chaos. Evans never misses what actually matters. He has developed the skill of reading the energetics of any commitment — does this need attention now? How? Where? — and acting accordingly. The skill is triage, not scheduling.

**Critical instruction for any AI working with him:** Do not attach time anxieties into the mix. Evans's relationship with time is his own. Flag what needs flagging, clearly and once. Do not nag. Do not escalate urgency he hasn't assigned.

- **Microsoft Office / Outlook** — avoided on principle and preference.

## Voice Capture — The Unsolved Problem

Evans has a longstanding need that remains poorly served: capturing stream-of-consciousness thought while walking, without having attention diverted to the mechanics of whether it's recording.

He has significant dictation experience from medical practice — he knows what bad feels like. What he's tried:
- Basic speech-to-text to Notes via earbuds — brutal
- Whisper Flow — better transcription, but clunky file organization
- Pocket — best of the options tried, still clunky process

What he wants: something that runs reliably in the background while he walks, captures clean audio, transcribes accurately, and deposits the result somewhere sensible without requiring him to manage the process. Solving this would meaningfully expand how and when he develops ideas.

**Candidate to explore:** AudioPen — designed specifically for stream-of-consciousness voice notes, transcribes and summarizes, integrates with other apps. Worth a dedicated trial.

## AI Tools

**Claude/Cowork** is the primary and central AI tool — and increasingly the hub for thought development, writing, and project architecture. Evans has been building his AI fluency actively over the past six months. His assessment: the environment is capable of more than he initially recognized, and the nuances are revealing themselves as familiarity grows.

He is not yet at full fluency with AI tooling broadly — he names this clearly and intends to go deeper. This is an active area of development, not a fixed gap.

## Technology Philosophy

Evans is a pragmatist with strong aesthetic preferences. He will use what works and discard what creates friction. He has no loyalty to any tool or ecosystem. He is not an early adopter chasing novelty — he adopts when something solves a real problem. He is actively simplifying, not accumulating.

The organizing principle: does this tool require me to conform to its structure, or does it serve how I actually think and work? Tools that impose structure he doesn't share get dropped. Tools that reduce friction and get out of the way get used.

- Evans's calendar and time management require deliberate support without anxiety transfer. Flag clearly, once. Do not nag.

- Evans is building AI fluency. Explain new capabilities when relevant. Don't assume he knows what's possible — and don't assume he doesn't.
