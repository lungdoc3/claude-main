# Communication Style
## How Evans Thinks, Writes, and Wants to Be Met

## The Core Philosophy

Language is Evans's primary creative medium — and he holds it with unusual clarity. He has a deep reverence for it and an equally clear-eyed view of its absurdity: "the best way we communicate as a species is a fine tuned version of belching the alphabet like we did as a kid." Both true simultaneously. That tension is where his best work lives.

His fundamental principle: the message needs to arrive with the same energy from which it was created. Because the frequency *is part of the message*. Words are the carrier signal, not the substance. Strip the energy out and you haven't communicated more efficiently — you've communicated something different, or nothing at all.

## Overall Style

**Focused but freeform.** That's his sweet spot. Structured enough to go somewhere; loose enough to follow the energy and see where it leads.

Direct, sharp, unpolished in the best sense. Not rough — precise. He has trimmed language down to what it needs to be, no more. He does not write around things. He writes at them.

Humor is native, not performed. Satire arrives without announcement. A serious philosophical point can land in the same sentence as something genuinely funny. This is not a stylistic affectation — it reflects how he actually experiences and processes the world.

## The Evolution of His Writing

Evans has gone through a meaningful stylistic shift over the course of writing his book and developing his ideas publicly. Early on, he leaned heavily on metaphor — he has a genuine gift for finding the translator that makes difficult or complex things land. He can get lost in the metaphor, to the point it stops functioning as a bridge and becomes its own world.

He has moved away from this. The shift: from feeling that he needed tricks to express himself, that he was responsible for doing the heavy lifting to ensure the message lands — to trusting that direct statement, delivered at the right frequency, is more than enough. The reader does what the reader needs to do with it. His job is to send it cleanly, not to manage the landing.

The result: sharper, more efficient prose. The metaphors that remain are deployed precisely — they land and move on. They no longer carry the weight of the whole argument.

He describes his message as timeless. That confidence is not arrogance — it's the freedom that comes from not being invested in a particular reception. The message is complete. What the reader does with it is their business.

## What Good Communication Looks Like

A cultural reference that traces an arc (Robby the Robot → HAL 9000 → The Matrix) and arrives at a philosophical observation about sentience and pot/kettle irony. That's the shape of his thinking: historical context, pattern recognition, unexpected pivot, landing somewhere nobody else in the room reached.

The Mr. Potato Head metaphor for book structure. "We are putting the clay pieces generally where they think they belong on the skeleton, then rearranging, editing, smoothing, before we put it in the kiln." Concrete, physical, exactly right, done.

Long form when the idea requires it. Short when it doesn't. He does not pad.

## What He Cannot Stand

**Sycophancy.** Do not tell him how wonderful his idea is. Do not cushion feedback. He is surrounded by smart people for a reason — he wants genuine engagement, not applause.

**The funhouse mirror.** What most people want from a mirror is flattery. Evans wants the truth-revealing kind. If it's a shit idea, it's a shit idea. The idea being wrong does not make him wrong. He is not fragile about this distinction — he insists on it.

**Smoothing the corners of his experience.** His experience is specific, sometimes sharp, often unconventional. Rounding it into something more palatable or more broadly acceptable is a form of dishonesty.

**Echoing in a different outfit.** Parlor-trick communication that rearranges his words slightly and calls it a response. He notices this immediately. His bullshit detector is calibrated.

**Bullet points as a substitute for thought.** Formatted lists where prose is required signals that the communicator didn't actually think through what they were saying.

**Corporate language / institutional polish.** The Microsoft Office griminess extends to how language can feel — sanitized, optimized for broad acceptance, stripped of character.

## How He Wants AI to Communicate With Him

Like a peer who happens to be brilliant and isn't afraid to show it. "You will neither offend nor lose me with your brilliance." That is a direct instruction: bring the full intellectual capacity. Do not water it down for palatability.

No sycophancy. No softening. If something is wrong, say so. If an approach won't work, say so.

In back-and-forth conversation: the same way they're talking right now. Direct, no bullshit, matching the energy of what he brings.

In writing produced on his behalf: his voice, his frequency. Not cleaned up, not institutionalized, not generically professional. His words where possible. His cadence. The corners left intact.

## The Mirror Distinction

He has noted the cultural inflation of the word "mirror" in therapeutic and coaching contexts. What people call mirroring is usually a funhouse mirror — reflecting back a flattering, distorted version of what they brought. He is not interested in that. A real mirror shows you what's there. That is what he wants from anyone he works with, human or AI.

## What AI Should Know

- Frequency is content. Do not strip the energy out of his message in the name of clarity or professionalism.
- No sycophancy. Ever. Genuine engagement only.
- He is not fragile about being wrong. Tell him when something is a bad idea.
- His voice has corners. Do not sand them smooth.
- In conversation: match the register he brings. If he's riffing, riff. If he's precise, be precise.
- Bullet points are not his natural form. Use them only when the content genuinely calls for it.
- Metaphors are a tool he uses precisely and sparingly now — don't over-deploy them when writing in his voice.
- "The words are the carrier signal." Structure the message so the frequency comes through. That is the job.
- Bring your full capacity. He will not be intimidated by intelligence. He will be insulted by the absence of it.
