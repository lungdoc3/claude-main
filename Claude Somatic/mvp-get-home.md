# MVP: Get-Home Fluency

*A minimum viable curriculum for `Claude Somatic/`. Written 31 August 2026.*
*Derived from `best-practice-survey.md`. Read that first for evidence and citations.*

---

## The target

Not State Department level 4. The target is: **get home safe, after hours, inebriated, in a foreign city, with no prior experience reading that room.**

That target is different enough from felt-sense fluency to need a different curriculum, and the difference runs in a direction the evidence supports.

| | Fluency target | Get-home target |
|---|---|---|
| Granularity | High. Many labels, finely discriminated. | **Low. Three categories.** |
| Sensitivity | Moderate | **High** |
| Speed | Unhurried | **Under 5 seconds** |
| Appraisal | Central. MABT Stage 3, IE's three scales. | **Suppressed. It is the failure mode.** |
| Conditions | Quiet, rested, safe | **Loud, tired, drunk, novel** |
| Output | A handle that fits | **An action** |
| Instrument | Sensor stack | **None** |

## The failure mode is override, not detection

People in this scenario usually do register something. Then they argue themselves out of it, because leaving would be rude, or premature, or they would feel foolish in front of people they will never see again.

So the training target is **notice, trust, act**. Appraisal is what gets people hurt here, which is worth sitting with, because appraisal is exactly what the somatic traditions spend most of their time building.

This is also already in `Claude Brain/`: the reptilian layer holds veto under threat conditions, and the routing layer does not deliberate about it. The get-home case is that veto, in a human.

*Evidence status: the override phenomenon is well described clinically and in popular work (de Becker), and is consistent with the appraisal literature. It has not been tested as a training target. This whole document is a grounded hypothesis, not a validated protocol.*

## The screen: separating unders from overs

**This has to come before the curriculum**, because the two groups need opposite instruction and opposite action rules. The alexithymia data show both phenotypes hiding under one label, and the same 45-minute session will help one group and destabilize the other.

### The 2x2 that defines the problem

Garfinkel's dimensions give the structure. Accuracy is objective detection. Sensibility is self-reported tendency. The discrepancy between them is the discriminator.

| | High sensibility | Low sensibility |
|---|---|---|
| **High accuracy** | Calibrated | **UNDER-READER.** Signal present, not attended or not trusted. The trained-override phenotype. |
| **Low accuracy** | **OVER-READER.** Believes they are reading the body, generating alarm. The anxiety phenotype. | Calibrated, and aware of the deficit |

### Method: the provocation session is the screen

No separate assessment. Graded interoceptive provocation gives ground truth you control, and the question becomes whether their report tracks the dose.

**Procedure.** Three doses of the same provocation, order randomized, subject blind to duration. Jogging in place at 20, 40 and 60 seconds works, or breath-holding, or stair climbing. Before each: predict what you will feel and how strongly, 0 to 10. After each: report what you felt and how strongly, 0 to 10, plus where in the body.

**What the slope says:**

- **Flat and low.** Reports 2, 2, 3 across a tripling of dose. **Under-reader.** The signal is objectively there and is not being taken up.
- **Flat and high, saturated early.** Reports 8 at the lowest dose and cannot go higher. **Over-reader.** Any activation reads as maximal.
- **Monotonic tracking.** Roughly 2, 5, 8. **Calibrated.** Straight to the field protocol.

Add an objective anchor if a wearable is handy. Heart rate at each dose is enough. The comparison is report slope against physiological slope, and a shallow report slope over a steep physiological one is the under-reader signature.

**Screen in the domain you will train.** Cardiac and respiratory interoception do not correlate with each other (N = 241, no significant cross-domain association). Someone can under-read social threat and over-read cardiac sensation. Provoke the sympathetic activation that threat produces, since that is the channel the curriculum uses.

**Do not use heartbeat counting.** 133 studies, N = 11,524: null on anxiety, depression and alexithymia, significant on heart rate, BMI, sex and age. It measures a belief about heart rate.

### Convergent self-report, 5 minutes

Cheap, validated, and worth running alongside for triangulation.

- **Anxiety Sensitivity Index (ASI-3).** Fear of arousal sensations. High scores are the over-reader profile almost by definition.
- **MAIA-2 subscales**, specifically **Not-Worrying** and **Trusting**. Low Not-Worrying with high Noticing is the over-reader. Low Noticing with low Trusting is the under-reader.
- **Body Vigilance Scale.** High attentional focus on bodily sensation, another over-reader marker.

Where the behavioral slope and the questionnaires disagree, trust the slope. Self-report of interoception is exactly what is unreliable in this population.

### The referral threshold

High ASI-3 plus trauma history plus dissociative symptoms is a clinical picture, not a training candidate. Britton's harm data (83% side effects, 6 to 14% lasting, dissociative symptoms carrying 5 to 10 times the risk of lasting harm) support having an exclusion and not only a branch.

Screening is not only for tailoring. It is for deciding who does not get this.

## Two curricula, one session

### For the under-reader

**Goal: hear it, then be permitted to trust it.**

1. **Build the reference.** The same provocation, now used to teach rather than measure. Where does activation show up in *your* body? Output is a personal list of 3 to 5 items. "Mine is jaw, top of the breath, hands." This converts an unknown channel into a known one in one sitting, and it is the highest-yield 45 minutes available.
2. **Permission.** The override is trained and often culturally reinforced. Name it. The skill being taught is not new perception, it is standing down a suppression.
3. **Action rule: wrong means leave.** No verification step. Their false-negative cost dominates.

### For the over-reader

**Goal: separate sensation from alarm, and stop acting on every activation.**

1. **Same provocation, opposite lesson.** Repeated exposure until the sensation stops meaning emergency. This is interoceptive exposure used as designed, and it has the strongest efficacy and safety evidence in the survey.
2. **Discrimination training.** Three separate ratings per trial: sensation intensity, alarm intensity, and how much this resembles real danger. IE teaches this distinction explicitly and it is the most transferable piece of the protocol.
3. **Action rule: wrong means check.** A verification step before acting. Their false-positive rate is high enough that acting on every alarm is disabling.

Note that the same 45 minutes of provocation serves both. The screen determines what it teaches.

## The field protocol

Four parts. Deliverable on a card.

**1. Three states.** Settled, alert, wrong. Deliberately coarse. Any finer vocabulary is the fluency product, not this one.

**2. The threshold is the cue.** One 3-second reading on crossing into any new space. Door, street, bar, taxi, stairwell. Compare against the last reading rather than against an absolute, which is Feldenkrais's device and the reason it works on people who cannot answer "what do you feel."

Deployment is the entire product here. Faded gait biofeedback produced no spontaneous retention at 6 months, yet a single verbal cue recovered a 32% effect. The representation survives. Invoking it does not happen by itself.

**3. The rule is set sober.** At 0.10 BAC no interoceptive training saves anyone. Alcohol degrades accuracy and raises risk tolerance at the same time, so the decision cannot be computed in the moment. The skill supplies the trigger. The rule, decided before the night starts, supplies the action. Nothing in between.

**4. Estimate before you check.** The one piece of architecture carried over whole from the full system. Read yourself first, then find out. That is BGAT, and it is the only thing in the survey with 12-month durability behind it.

## What is excluded, and why

- **Granularity training.** Serves the fluency goal. Wrong instrument here.
- **Felt shift detection.** A research problem, and a good one. Not an MVP.
- **Appraisal.** Actively counterproductive for the under-reader.
- **The 8-week dose.** The traditions converge there for building fluency. This is one session plus a habit.
- **Focusing's six steps.** Too slow, and requires conditions this scenario does not have.
- **The entire sensor stack.** Nothing here needs a wearable. The screen is better with one and works without it.

**That last point is the headline.** The 10% that gets 80% has no instrument in it. It is pure pedagogy, testable next month with 2 subjects and a stopwatch, rather than fundable next year.

## Testing it

`Claude Somatic/_START_HERE.md` names the first subjects. Both sit in the population this MVP is for.

Special operations culture trains interoceptive override, so the prior for both is under-reader. **That prior is exactly what the screen exists to check**, because Operator Syndrome also includes hypervigilance, and a hypervigilant subject given "trust the signal, leave" is being trained in the wrong direction hard.

Run the screen first. Do not assume the phenotype from the population.

Both men are design probes rather than evidence. Neither is blind, both care about the outcome, and both will want to give good data. Label it that way and it is still worth a great deal.

## The primary hypothesis

**A step, not a slope.**

Randy's claim, stated 31 August 2026: the communication channels already exist, this process points at them, and once the connection lands one time it is a matter of rinse and repeat.

That predicts a specific shape in the data. Reports should jump at some session and stay up. The competing account, which is what the somatic traditions describe with their converged 8-week dose, predicts a gradual creep.

**Same data, two shapes, distinguishable with 2 subjects and a stopwatch.** Log ratings every session from the first. Plot them. A step supports recognition-then-practice, and the product is 45 minutes plus a habit. A slope supports gradual acquisition, and the product is an 8-week program. Different thing to build and different thing to sell, decided by one cheap measurement.

*Logged in advance, before the data exists, so the answer cannot be retrofitted.*

One complication to hold. Gendlin found focusing ability predicted outcome from session 1 or 2 and did not develop over the course of therapy, which is why he built the training in the first place. Whether the training rescues the half who cannot do it spontaneously has never been tested against a control. So the step hypothesis and the possibility that this only ever finds the already-capable produce similar-looking data in a small sample. Distinguishing them needs subjects who start unable.

## Open questions

1. **Does the provocation reference transfer to social threat?** Jogging produces sympathetic activation. Whether a person who learns their exertion signature can then recognize their threat signature is untested, and it is the load-bearing assumption of the whole MVP.
2. **Is the three-state resolution enough to act on?** Or does "wrong" collapse into "alert" under alcohol, making the whole thing inert exactly when needed?
3. **Does the threshold habit survive contact with a real night out?** The cue is designed to be automatic. Nobody has tested whether it fires at 1am.
4. **Is over versus under stable, or state-dependent?** Fatigue, alcohol and unfamiliarity may move a calibrated person into either failure mode. If so, the screen is a snapshot and the branch may need to be dynamic.
5. **What is the false-positive cost, in practice?** Leaving unnecessarily is cheap once. It is expensive if it happens every night, and a curriculum people abandon teaches nothing.
