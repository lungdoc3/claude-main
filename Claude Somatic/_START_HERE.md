## The Premise

We have not updated the nature of the carbon / silicon system relationship since the 1960s. The interface in front of us was designed by people who were brilliant about computers and mostly not literate about brains. Each side got optimized in isolation. The unasked question: how do we optimize each system to interact with the other?

---

## The Chain of Thought

Listening to Del describe his harness is what set this off. Very few of the OG computer geeks were brain-literate.

**1. Output design is the low-hanging fruit.**
Every data output looks the same. A window filled with lots and lots of information streams, more than the carbon system can use effectively. The move runs backwards from the usual direction: study how the carbon system manages inputs, then gear the output to meet those input requirements. Design for the receiver.

**2. The agent structure.**
A multi-agent system functioning something like the Quadriune brain. Holarchical, each agent meeting the world from a deliberately limited perspective (core survival, relational and social, logic), with an integration logic established over the top.

**3. Measuring the felt sense state.**
What do we have access to? HRV, voice analysis, facial action units and emotion detection, pupillary reactions, and more. The affective computing industry (iMotions and similar) built this instrument stack to evaluate advertising effectiveness.

**4. The delta use case.**
If the system can predict the decision Randy would usually make in an optimized state, the gap between optimal decision and made decision measures decision-ability degradation. Hangry. Emotionally challenged. Something is degrading the carbon system. Past a threshold, the system stops and points out that his brain is mush.

**5. All of which requires fluency in the felt sense language.**
Seems obvious once stated. Few people know this communication channel exists, much less speak it fluently.

**6. Which exposed a gap in the book.**
*Healing with Psychedelics* had not spoken to the felt sense directly. Now written.

**7. Which opens the education problem.**
Somatic education. Apps, games, whatever actually teaches the channel. A close friend, a functional medicine specialist and the queen of the somatic signal, is the first collaboration conversation. Opened 30 August 2026.

## The Correction

The education problem felt sticky and difficult for a long time. The stickiness was the signal that it was the wrong question.

What steps 3 through 7 were quietly proposing: a silicon system learns the carbon-based felt sense language, so that it can then teach that language back to the carbon system. Second-hand knowledge, derived from external proxies, instructing the only system with direct interior access to the signal.

That is absurd from the get-go, and it takes breaking down to see it.

The correct approach leans into what each system is actually good at. **The carbon system is good at the felt sense.** It is native there. So instead of assigning meaning, or even associating meaning, the work is to turn the mirror onto the carbon system and draw attention to the presence of the signal. Real-world examples. Conscious thought experiments. Whatever the somatic training world already uses.

**What survives the correction:** detection. Silicon notices that something changed, continuously, tirelessly, at resolutions and timescales carbon cannot consciously sample. That is a genuine silicon strength and it requires no interpretation.

**What dies:** interpretation. The machine does not say what the signal means. Ever.

This is neurofeedback where the signal is the felt sense and the completion is the alignment between the person's conceptualization and the actual message. The completion event is interior. Only the carbon system has it.

## Where The Instruments Sit

*Answered 31 August 2026 by `best-practice-survey.md` in this folder. Read that file for evidence and citations.*

**The question was wrong.** It was posed as timing and frequency: how often should the display appear, and how close to the moment. Frequency is not the causal variable. A 2022 meta-analysis (75 effect sizes, N = 2,228) found no benefit to reduced feedback frequency at any timepoint and no trace of the predicted reversal. Faded schedules failed as a moderator. The authors told practitioners to stop taking recommendations on feedback frequency.

**The causal variable is whether the learner commits to a guess before the reading arrives.** In a 2x2 crossing error estimation with feedback frequency, 100% feedback was the *best* condition when the learner had to commit to an internal judgment first, and the *worst* condition when they did not. Frequency was a noisy proxy for whether error estimation was happening at all, which is why frequency effects meta-analyze to zero.

So the probe-first ordering in The System Test below is correct, for a reason this file previously got wrong. The reason is not that instruments in the room invite intellectualizing. The reason is that a reading arriving before a committed guess removes the only event that produces learning.

**A second literature reaches the same place by a different route.** Specificity of practice: more practice under vision made movements *more* dependent on vision, not less. Learning does not migrate out of the sensory conditions of acquisition. If the goal state is unaided interoception, a substantial share of practice must occur unaided.

**What remains genuinely open.** Concurrent feedback benefits learning on complex tasks via load reduction, and degrades it on simple ones. The felt sense is motorically trivial and perceptually hard, and the moderator was derived from motor complexity. A display may be needed early to solve the discrimination problem, which sensation is the target. That job is short and ends as soon as the learner can find the signal.

**Fade on measured accuracy, never on a schedule.** Withdrawal pace must match current expertise; adaptive fading beats fixed fading. This system generates its own fading signal for free: the running error between the learner's committed estimate and the sensor reading. Collect subjective effort ratings alongside accuracy, which is what separates real schema development from performance that merely looks adequate.

**The warning that should carry most weight.** Under cockpit automation, pilots' psychomotor skills survived four months of disuse largely intact. Their interpretive skills collapsed: reading state from raw indications, knowing what follows. Only 1 of 16 navigated error-free without the map display. Degradation correlated with mind-wandering during automated flight, meaning a display does not only substitute for the skill, it withdraws attention from the signal. **This project trains an interpretive skill. Passive exposure with a display running does not count as practice.**

**Retention and deployment are separate problems.** Faded gait biofeedback produced no spontaneous retention at 6 months, yet a single verbal cue recovered a 32% reduction in impact loading. The representation was intact and durable. The learner never invoked it. Fading addresses retention. Only cue and context design addresses deployment.

**Corollary, still standing:** sampling may want to be event-triggered rather than clock-triggered, since scheduled pings are a direct assault on the world of no time. Note the tension: the granularity statistic requires signal-contingent sampling. A parallel event-contingent stream, analyzed separately, is the compromise.

## The Annie Sullivan Role

The useful picture is not the water pump scene. It is what comes after, once Helen has the superpower, is uppity with it, and is careening around demanding the name of everything, while Sullivan follows her as bumper and buffer.

Sullivan is not having the experience. Helen is. Sullivan supplies structure on demand, catches the collision Helen is too absorbed to see coming, and mediates with a world that cannot keep up. She spells relentlessly into the hand and lets Helen take what she can use.

Where the analogy inverts, and it matters: Sullivan had access to a referent world Helen lacked. Here the carbon system has the privileged access and the silicon does not. The narrow exception is the one thing worth building on. Silicon can see the drift across 6 weeks that nobody can feel from the inside. **Annie Sullivan for the slow signals.**

Worth noting that the movie ends at the pump. Everybody tells the breakthrough story. Nobody tells the long calibration story that follows, which is the entire product.

## The First Subjects

Randy is a poor test subject at both ends. Ceiling on the felt sense (decades of ayahuasca work, breathwork, thousands of deaths at the bedside) and floor on predictability (he follows energy rather than clock time, and names the drive to not be predictably predictable as the strongest force in his life). Every design decision in this folder so far has been calibrated against an atypical instrument.

**Del is the felt-sense naive subject.** Not as a convenience sample standing in for an ordinary user, but as n = 1 of the actual clinical target. Special operations culture systematically trains people to override interoceptive signal: push through it, ignore it, function anyway. That override is part of what Operator Syndrome is. A subject from that world is the population `Claude Brain/` and APEX already exist to serve.

The symmetry is worth noticing. Del was the test case for whether the Quadriune framework was teachable. He is now the test case for whether the felt sense is. Same man, both proofs of concept.

**Randy's son is the truer sample.** Del is adjacent to the population. His son is in it: active Green Beret, Harvard physics, five languages. Two subjects at different distances from the target is better design than either alone. Del is the builder who has to hold the model in his head. His son is the operator the model is ultimately about.

One thing to watch on the vocabulary bottleneck. Physics training and five languages mean unusually high verbal precision available for a channel he may be entirely naive to. High linguistic capacity paired with low interoceptive vocabulary is a combination none of the traditions describe, and it may produce handles that are articulate and wrong, which is harder to detect than handles that are absent.

**Label all of it honestly.** Neither man is blind to the hypothesis, both care about Randy, and both will want to give good data. That makes them design probes, not evidence. Design probes from inside the target population are still worth a great deal, and worth more than another iteration calibrated against Randy's own interior.

**And name the thing that makes his son different from any other subject.** What this system measures is degradation under load, in a profession where degradation kills people. Randy is not a neutral investigator there. Decide in advance what happens if the instrument says something he does not want to know, because that decision should not get made for the first time while looking at the data.

**What this implies for scope.** Write down who the system is not for. Most people have grooves and a baseline exists for them. Randy may be outside the population his own delta engine assumes, and naming that boundary now beats discovering it in a trial later.

## The Method

Two existing bodies of work, developed on separate tracks, that fit together.

**Focusing generates the labels.** Gendlin's protocol produces idiosyncratic, person-specific handles for felt senses, and explicitly resists standardized vocabulary on the grounds that a borrowed category word papers over the felt sense instead of matching it. It is a tested pedagogy, designed to be taught to non-professionals in pairs. See `gendlin-primer.md` in this folder.

**Granularity measures them.** Emotional granularity (Barrett's lineage) is computed as how much a person's own labels covary within themselves. Labels that move as a block do no differentiating work. Labels that dissociate do. The statistic is computed within one person, compares them to nobody, and never asks whether any label is correct.

That within-subject property is the requirement, not a convenience. BrainAdvantage extinguished any thought of a standard. Every attempt to build one flattens the individual into an average that describes nobody.

**What this project can add that neither has.** Classical granularity is vulnerable to vocabulary sophistication: a person with a large feelings lexicon can produce nicely differentiated labels that do not correspond to differentiated states. Literary rather than real. With a sensor stack you can check whether the handles track distinct physiological patterns. That is a methodological upgrade available to almost nobody, and it is a study.

## The System Test

The loop that turns the monitor into a teacher, rewritten after the correction so that the machine never supplies meaning.

1. Silicon detects a change and says nothing about what it is.
2. Silicon issues a content-free prompt. Something is happening. Read yourself.
3. Carbon produces a handle, in its own words, plus a confidence.
4. Silicon confirms only *that* the signal was present and when. No interpretation, no reveal of a machine read.
5. What gets scored over time: detection latency, whether the handles differentiate, and whether the differentiation tracks physiology.

**Two modes.** Holding a degradation flag to preserve a clean probe is fine for a low-stakes moment and wrong for a high-stakes one. Guardian mode flags immediately and says only that performance is drifting, which is a statement about performance rather than about meaning. Training mode probes first. Context picks the mode, and the stakes threshold is a design decision to make on purpose.

**Sample adaptively.** Probe hardest where historical calibration is worst. Some states arrive with inflated self-assessment built in, and those are worth 10 ordinary probes. The blind spots are the curriculum. This is also why the bumper exists: new fluency breeds overconfidence, and high confidence paired with low accuracy is the dangerous quadrant that by definition cannot be self-diagnosed.

**The chase.** Gendlin describes the felt shift as a distinct bodily event: a release, often an exhale, a postural drop. If it has a physiological signature, the machine can detect *that a shift occurred* while staying blind to *what shifted*. The loop closes with no semantic authority anywhere in it, and time-to-shift becomes a clean training metric.

**Population note.** Decades of ayahuasca work, breathwork, and thousands of deaths at the bedside put Randy near the ceiling on this capacity. Good designer, poor test subject. The education product serves people at the floor, where probe cadence, vocabulary, and failure modes all look different. Probably two instruments.

## Open Questions

1. **Ground truth for "optimal."** Where does the optimal label come from, given that *usual* includes every degraded decision already made?

   *Working resolution.* There may be no single base state, and looking for one may be the error. Night is prime creative time, mid-afternoon is dead air, and one baseline averages them into a person who does not exist. Condition the expectation on context (circadian phase, time since food, social load, task type) and the delta measures against the nearest matching prior instead of a fiction.

   *On the observer problem.* The labeler is both a requirement and a guarantee that the state is no longer the base state. If the system runs continuously, though, watched becomes the operating condition, no decision gets made in the unwatched state again, and observation is constant across every stored state so it cancels out of the comparison. Design implications: continuous and silent rather than episodic and announced, since reactivity to self-monitoring habituates; and measurement separated from labeling, so the act of labeling never lands inside the moment being labeled.

2. **What is current best practice for training this skill?** **ANSWERED** by `best-practice-survey.md`, 31 August 2026. Headlines: six independent traditions agree that nobody says "just notice your body" (every one supplies an amplifier or anchor first), that vocabulary is taught rather than assumed, that contrast beats absolute detection, and that dose converges on 8 weekly sessions. MABT is the manualized model worth adapting. Blood Glucose Awareness Training is this project's architecture already built and validated in medicine, with 12-month durability. And Gendlin named the instrument specification in 1978 (sigh, facial loosening, postural relaxation) and ran a GSR study of the felt-sense mode in 1961 that has never been replicated.

3. **Specificity of the signal.** Arousal reads as arousal. Hunger, caffeine, anger, and excitement can look alike across HRV and facial action units. Under the corrected frame this may not matter, since the machine only needs to say *something is here* and the carbon system supplies the rest.

4. **Does the delta engine still belong in this project?** Detecting performance degradation requires no interpretation of meaning, so it may be a separate product on its own track while the education work goes the Focusing route. Two products that share a sensor stack.

5. **The authority gradient.** When the system says performance is drifting and the carbon system disagrees, who wins? Aviation had to answer this after crews died deferring to captains who were wrong. The answer shapes the interface.

6. **Where the body enters the Quadriune model.** The reptilian layer in `Claude Brain/` does threat and survival with no afferent body attached to it. The somatic channel may be the missing input, which would make these two projects one project. **The populations may also converge:** APEX targets Operator Syndrome in special operations forces, and trained interoceptive override is part of that picture. If the somatic work serves the same people, these are one project on two axes rather than two projects sharing an architecture.

7. **Has anyone put physiological instrumentation on Focusing sessions?** **ANSWERED: no.** Verified absence across English and Japanese sources, TIFI's own research pages, and its roughly 100-dissertation database spanning 13 countries and 1971 to 2024. The systematic review of interpersonal physiology in psychotherapy does not mention Gendlin, Focusing, or experiential therapy at all. The two literatures have never intersected. Exceptions: Gendlin & Berlin 1961 (GSR, N = 17, never replicated), its unpublished companion, one unverified 1977 EEG report, and Rieko Kurino in Japan, the only active researcher on this. The study is small, cheap and fundable. See `best-practice-survey.md` section 6.

8. **New, from the survey.** Screening and branching for hypo- versus hyper-perceivers (the alexithymia relationship is quadratic, not linear). Active harm monitoring with a dissociation protocol that overrides "just notice it." A sampled-but-untrained control arm, because measurement alone raises emotional clarity. Explicit defense against base-rate inference, which is the specific way this design will produce apparent skill that is not perceptual. And the granularity index itself is more fragile than assumed: children score high on it, which vocabulary accounts cannot explain.
